An Evening of Song,
Built Around Your Room

From an intimate dinner party on the Upper East Side to a black tie gala at Cipriani, I put together each set around the room, the guests, and the occasion. Here's how we usually work together.

01

Private Events & Dinner Parties

A trio or a solo piano set for a home, a rooftop, or a private dining room. I'll build the program around your guests and the tempo of the evening.

02

Corporate Functions & Galas

Holiday parties, award dinners, product launches. I know how to hold a room during dinner and bring the energy up when it's time to dance.

03

Weddings & Engagements

Ceremony, cocktail hour, first dance. We'll talk through your favorite songs ahead of time so the day sounds like the two of you.

04

Club & Concert Dates

Guest appearances and one night engagements outside of my residencies, for venues and promoters looking for a jazz and standards vocalist with a full book of arrangements.
