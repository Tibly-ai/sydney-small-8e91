Two Rooms, Two Nights

Where to Hear Sydney

Sydney Small holds two standing residencies in Manhattan, each with its own room, its own crowd, and its own hour of the evening. Both are open to the public. Reservations are recommended.

Residency No. 1

The Flatiron Room

Thursdays, 8:30pm to 11:30pm

37 W 26th St, New York, NY

Downstairs in the whiskey library, Sydney leads a trio through the American songbook, deep cuts from the 1940s, and a torch song or two saved for last call. The room is low-lit and close. Bring someone you want to sit near.

Reserve a Table

Residency No. 2

Cipriani NYC

First Friday of the month, 7pm to 10pm

55 Wall St, New York, NY

Once a month under Cipriani's marble columns, Sydney opens the evening with a set built for the room: Italian standards, big band swing, and a few requests if the piano player knows them. It is grander, later, and worth dressing up for.
