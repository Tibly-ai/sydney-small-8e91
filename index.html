A Voice for the Room

Old-world glamour, sung the way it was meant to be heard.

I grew up on the Great American Songbook and never left it. Six nights a week you can find me at a piano bar or a supper club somewhere in Manhattan, working a room the way singers did before microphones did all the heavy lifting. My sets move from Gershwin to Sinatra to the occasional torch song nobody remembers the writer of anymore, and I tailor every evening to the crowd in front of me.
