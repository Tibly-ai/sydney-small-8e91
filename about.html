About Sydney

The Voice Behind
the Room

Sydney Small learned to sing before she learned to read music, standing on a choir riser in her grandmother's Baptist church in Newark, New Jersey. By thirteen she had memorized every Ella Fitzgerald record in the house. By nineteen she was singing standards for tips in Greenwich Village basement clubs that no longer exist.

She studied jazz voice at the Manhattan School of Music, then spent the better part of a decade paying dues. Cocktail hours in Murray Hill. Weddings in New Jersey banquet halls. A six month stretch singing on a cruise ship out of Miami. Every room taught her something about holding an audience's attention without a spotlight.

In 2017, a chance sit in at the Flatiron Room turned into a standing Thursday night booking. Cipriani followed two years later, first for a single private event, then a recurring evening slot that has become one of the most requested nights on their calendar. She has been in residence at both rooms ever since, building a following of regulars who plan their evenings around her sets.
